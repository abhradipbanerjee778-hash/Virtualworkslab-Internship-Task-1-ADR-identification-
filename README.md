## Overview

This project documents a case-based assessment of a published report titled *“Amoxicillin-Clavulanate-Induced Pityriasis Rosea: A Pharmacovigilance Case Report.”* It identifies the suspected medicine and adverse drug reaction (ADR), summarizes the patient and medication history, records the clinical timeline, and discusses the evidence supporting the drug–reaction relationship.

## Contents

- `PV-Task-1-ADR-Identification.pdf` — Complete Task 1 submission.

## Key topics covered

- Patient demographics and medication history
- Suspected drug and reported skin reaction
- Clinical timeline and reaction outcome
- Temporal association, dechallenge, rechallenge, and alternative causes
- Preliminary causality assessment
- Case summary, conclusion, and reference
